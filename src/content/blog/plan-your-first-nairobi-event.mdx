So you want to put on your first event in Nairobi. Whether it is a 200-capacity
comedy night in Westlands or a 5,000-person festival at Carnivore Grounds, the
fundamentals are the same: lock the venue early, know your numbers, and make
buying a ticket effortless.

## 1. Lock your venue eight to twelve weeks out

Nairobi's best venues book out fast, especially around public holidays and
school breaks. When comparing venues, ask for:

- **Capacity by zone** — standing versus seated numbers differ wildly
- **Sound and power** — does the PA come included, or do you hire separately?
- **Curfew and licensing** — Nairobi County noise regulations bite after 10 PM
  in residential areas
- **Load-in windows** — a 2 PM load-in can make or break your schedule

## 2. Budget backwards from your ticket price

Work out your break-even before you print a single poster. A useful rule of
thumb for a first-time organiser:

> Break-even tickets = (venue + production + marketing) / (average ticket price - platform fee)

On TicketNest, buyers pay a flat KES 100 service fee per ticket and organisers
pay a 5% platform fee on payouts, so build those into your model from day one.

## 3. Price in tiers, not in guesses

Early Bird, Regular and VIP is the classic ladder for a reason. Early Bird
tickets (15 to 20% below Regular) reward your core fans and give you early
cash flow. Keep VIP scarce, around 5 to 10% of capacity, and bundle real
value: dedicated gate, seating, a welcome drink.

## 4. Make paying painless

If your checkout asks for anything more than a name, phone and M-Pesa PIN,
you are losing sales. M-Pesa STK push should be the default path, with card
payment as the fallback for diaspora buyers. Send tickets to both email and
WhatsApp the moment payment clears, because screenshots of PDFs are how most
tickets are actually shared in Kenya.

## 5. Control the door with QR scanning

Paper guest lists break at 400 people. Signed QR codes scanned at the gate
give you live attendance counts, stop double entry and let you transfer
tickets legitimately instead of fighting screenshot fraud.

## 6. Market where your crowd already is

Instagram and TikTok carry discovery in Nairobi, but WhatsApp carries
conversion. Give every ticket buyer a one-tap share link and consider a
WhatsApp status discount code for the final 72 hours before the event.

---

Plan well, price honestly, and keep the buying flow short. The crowd takes
care of the rest. See you at the gate.
