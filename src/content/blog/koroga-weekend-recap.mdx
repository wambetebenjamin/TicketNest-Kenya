Three cities. Five headline acts. 22,000 scanned QR codes. The first weekend
of October was the biggest in TicketNest's short history, and the numbers
tell a story every East African organiser should hear.

## Nairobi: the block party that sold out in 36 hours

The Gengetone Block Party at Ngong Racecourse moved 3,500 tickets, with the
final 1,800 selling in the last 36 hours before gates opened. Early Bird sold
out in under two days. Average time from checkout page to paid ticket:
**41 seconds**, with 93% of buyers choosing M-Pesa STK push.

## Kisumu: laughter by the lake

Lakeside Laughs proved regional comedy is a headline genre, not a warm-up.
The VIP Laugh Tables, a KES 2,000 tier including a welcome drink and photos
with the acts, sold out a full week before General entry, a first for a
Kisumu comedy show on the platform.

## Mombasa: families buy in bundles

The Coast Classic Derby's family bundles outsold single tickets two to one.
When we say East African audiences plan in groups, we mean it: 61% of Derby
orders were for two or more tickets.

## What we learned

- **WhatsApp delivery is king.** 78% of buyers who received their ticket via
  WhatsApp tapped the share button within an hour.
- **Transfers are trust builders.** Buyers who transferred a ticket to a
  friend were more likely to buy again within 30 days than those who did not.
- **The last 72 hours decide everything.** Across all three events, 46% of
  total ticket sales happened after the 72-hour countdown posts started.
